# Ramon Miguel Angeles: Composer

Hello, and welcome to my little corner of the internet—my digital garden! This website still a work in progress. I'm figuring things out, adding things, and letting it grow over time. 

I am Ramon Miguel Angeles, a Filipino composer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Music in Music Composition with a specialization in Music Production and Engineering at the Centro Escolar University (CEU) Conservatory of Music. My musical interests are broad: I am drawn to concert works, film and game scores, atonal and experimental music, and music from different cultures. I am still exploring these interests and developing my own voice as a composer.

This website serves both as a home for my work as a composer and as a growing collection of my writing, research, notes, and ideas. Thank you for taking the time to read and visit!
